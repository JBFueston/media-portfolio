Finally had my own drone to take up at the cabin. Spent a battery or two toying with the drone along the property and out over the lake. The DJI Mini 4k is by no means a top of the line model, but compared to the Latraxx and home-built arducopter models it is remarkable. Have never had the confidence to fly out so high, so far, or over water and got some good practice of smooth, sweeping shots, handling near obstacles and at low altitudes, and FPV flight.

### Flight Notes
* **Conditions:** Clear
* **Max Altitude:** 400 ft
* **Camera Settings:** 4K 30fps Auto
