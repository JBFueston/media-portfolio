Contacted by a friend to take some drone shots for Alpha Xi Delta at Embry-Riddle to be used in their Fall 2026 recruiting video. Got to practice smooth manual tracking of vehicles and groups of people as well as the workflow for a real drone photography job. Planned to launch and land in/out of the truck bed, which wasn't always an option, so the drone had to either land on a beach towel or idle in the air. Next investment would be additional batteries and a real portable launch pad.

The biggest challenge here was on the first day of the shoot, where I was tracking the Jeep while being more or less blind to what was behind the drone. Power lines, varying treeline, and moderate winds made it a little tricky. I would have liked to have been able to get lower and closer to the jeep as it was driving but at our specific location I don't believe it would be possible without flying directly over the roadway. 4k should be high enough resolution to punch in and resolve this issue.

Ormund Loop location was a beautiful example of Florida low country, and I'd love to go back at some point to get some shots of my own over the flats.

The second day was fairly simple as we were at the beach. There wasn't any obstacles and there was no need for high-speed tracking. The drone handled the wind suprisingly well and it really did all of the heavy lifting. The shots turned out well but it was a much less technical shoot. The view down the coast is incredible.

### Flight Notes
* **Conditions:** Windy
* **Max Altitude:** 150 ft
* **Camera Settings:** 4K 30fps Auto
